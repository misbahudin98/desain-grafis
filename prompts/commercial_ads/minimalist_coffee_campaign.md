## 1. PROMPT MIDJOURNEY v6.1

```
High-end commercial advertising photography of an amber glass bottle of cold brew coffee labeled "KALA COFFEE" in clean Swiss typography. The bottle stands on a wet black textured granite rock in a shallow stream of crystal-clear water with subtle ripples. Soft morning mist drifting in the background forest. Dynamic lighting with a diffused warm sunbeam cutting through tree branches, backlighting the amber liquid into a radiant golden caramel glow, while a fill reflector illuminates the crisp minimalist label. Shot on Sony A1 with 90mm f/2.8 Macro lens at f/4. High shutter speed capturing crisp suspended micro-water droplets, authentic glass refraction, rich contrast, Kinfolk editorial aesthetic --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 6.1
```
