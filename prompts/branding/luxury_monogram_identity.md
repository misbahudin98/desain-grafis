# Master Prompt: Luxury Monogram Brand Identity
> **Kategori:** Branding & Visual Identity  
> **Target Engine:** Midjourney v6.1 / FLUX.1  
> **Konsep:** Monogram identitas mewah minimalis untuk firma hukum & arsitektur.

---

## 1. PROMPT MIDJOURNEY v6.1

```
Minimalist luxury architectural brand identity presentation of a bespoke embossed monogram logo on heavy 600gsm raw cotton paper stock. The debossed foil monogram features ultra-precise Swiss geometric lines in muted bronze. The stationery set includes an envelope, a minimalist business card, and a brass ruler arranged on a honed dark graphite marble table. Soft diffused morning daylight from a large window at a 45-degree angle, casting long subtle soft-edge shadows. Shot on Hasselblad H6D-100c, 80mm lens at f/4. Tactile paper fibers visible, microscopic foil stamping impression depth, quiet luxury aesthetic, elegant negative space, photorealistic --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```

## 2. PROMPT FLUX.1 (DEV / SCHNELL)

```
A high-end editorial flatlay photograph of a luxury corporate stationery suite. In the center, a thick black textured business card with clean gold embossed foil typography that reads "VAULT & CO.". Beside the card lies a solid titanium ballpoint pen and a folded letterhead on cream laid paper with deckle edges. The items are neatly arranged according to a Swiss modular grid on a warm neutral limestone slab. Lighting is soft, indirect daylight coming from the upper left, creating gentle realistic drop shadows. Photographed with a 50mm prime lens at f/5.6, hyper-detailed texture of the paper grain, crisp sharp metallic reflections, zero distortions, sophisticated minimalist design.
```
