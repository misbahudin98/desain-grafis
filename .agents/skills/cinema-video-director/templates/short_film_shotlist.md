## CONTOH PROYEK: "THE LAST ARCHIVIST" (DURASI 30 DETIK / 4 SHOT)

### PENGANTAR PROYEK
- **Logline:** Di sebuah perpustakaan bawah tanah pada era pasca-digital, seorang arsiparis tua berusaha menyelamatkan satu-satunya buku kertas terakhir yang tersisa.
- **Visual Style:** Sinematik Chiaroscuro, Kodak Vision3 500T, palet tungsten amber & cyan dalam, Arri Alexa LF dengan lensa anamorphic.

---

### SHOT 01 — ESTABLISHING THE SANCTUARY (ELS)
- **Tipe Bidikan:** Extreme Long Shot (ELS)
- **Sudut Kamera:** High-angle slightly looking down
- **Gerakan Kamera:** Slow forward crane tracking down along the towering bookshelves
- **Durasi Target:** 5 detik
- **Deskripsi Adegan:** Perpustakaan kuno bawah tanah dengan rak buku kayu setinggi 20 meter. Cahaya keemasan lilin kecil tampak berkedip di kejauhan tempat seorang pria kecil bertubuh bungkuk sedang duduk di meja kayu besar. Partikel debu menari di udara.
- **Runway Gen-3 Prompt:**  
  `[Camera]: High angle slow crane descending diagonally forward through the massive cavernous library. [Scene]: Vast ancient subterranean archive with endless towering bookshelves disappearing into shadow, single glowing desk lamp in the far distance, dust particles swirling in warm golden shafts of volumetric light, Arri Alexa Mini LF, 24mm wide lens, cinematic depth of field, 24fps.`
- **Kling 1.5 Prompt:**  
  `Cinematic wide establishing shot of an ancient subterranean library, massive towering wooden bookshelves, dust motes floating in golden light shafts, camera smoothly craning down and pushing forward, photorealistic, 4k resolution, Kodak Vision3 film stock, no flickering.`

---

### SHOT 02 — THE PRECIOUS ARTIFACT (MCU)
- **Tipe Bidikan:** Medium Close-Up (MCU)
- **Sudut Kamera:** Eye-level
- **Gerakan Kamera:** Slow lateral truck right tracking the movement
- **Durasi Target:** 5 detik
- **Deskripsi Adegan:** Seorang arsiparis berusia 70 tahun dengan kacamata berbingkai kuningan bulat membalik halaman buku kertas kulit kuno dengan sangat hati-hati. Wajahnya diterangi pantulan cahaya hangat dari halaman buku.
- **Runway Gen-3 Prompt:**  
  `[Camera]: Smooth lateral truck right at eye level, maintaining focus on the subject. [Scene]: Weathered 70-year-old archivist with round brass spectacles carefully turning the yellowed deckle-edged paper page of an antique leather-bound book, warm amber candlelight illuminating his focused eyes and gentle wrinkles, Zeiss Master Prime 50mm f/1.4, creamy bokeh, authentic paper texture.`
- **Kling 1.5 Prompt:**  
  `Medium close-up of an elderly scholar with brass spectacles delicately turning an ancient book page, camera tracking right smoothly, warm tungsten lighting, intimate mood, ultra-detailed skin and paper fiber, 24fps.`

---

### SHOT 03 — DETAIL OF PRESERVATION (ECU)
- **Tipe Bidikan:** Extreme Close-Up (ECU)
- **Sudut Kamera:** Top-down angled at 45 degrees
- **Gerakan Kamera:** Slow micro push-in forward
- **Durasi Target:** 4 detik
- **Deskripsi Adegan:** Ujung jari tua bergetar menyentuh goresan kaligrafi tinta emas timbul pada lembaran perkamen. Tinta berkilau lembut saat cahaya lilin bergoyang tertiup angin pelan.
- **Runway Gen-3 Prompt:**  
  `[Camera]: Micro push-in macro shot with shallow depth of field. [Scene]: An aged weathered fingertip gently grazing the embossed gold leaf calligraphy on parchment paper, flickering candlelight catching the metallic gold flakes, 100mm macro lens at f/2.8, microscopic tactile texture, photorealistic.`
- **Kling 1.5 Prompt:**  
  `Extreme close up macro shot of an aged finger touching gold calligraphy on old parchment, gentle slow zoom in, warm flickering candle reflections, hyper-realistic, 1080p, 24fps.`

---

### SHOT 04 — THE OMINOUS INTERRUPTION (MS TO CU)
- **Tipe Bidikan:** Medium Shot transitioning into Close-Up via Dolly In
- **Sudut Kamera:** Low-angle looking up
- **Gerakan Kamera:** Dynamic rapid push-in (dolly in) with slight tilt up
- **Durasi Target:** 4 detik
- **Deskripsi Adegan:** Sang arsiparis tiba-tiba mengangkat kepalanya, tatapan matanya waspada. Dari balik pintu besi besar di belakangnya, bayangan biru dingin dan desisan mekanis terdengar mendekat. Cahaya lilin padam tertiup angin kencang.
- **Runway Gen-3 Prompt:**  
  `[Camera]: Dramatic low-angle fast dolly-in pushing abruptly into the archivist's tense face. [Scene]: Elderly archivist suddenly freezing and looking up towards the heavy vault door, cold cyan mechanical searchlights slicing through the shadows behind him, candle flame blowing out leaving drifting smoke, anamorphic lens flare, high tension.`
- **Kling 1.5 Prompt:**  
  `Low-angle cinematic shot, rapid dolly in to elderly man's face looking terrified, cold blue light bursting from background vault door, candle blowing out, smoke curling, dramatic Hollywood suspense lighting, 24fps.`
